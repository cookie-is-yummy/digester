# Experiment log

One row per experiment. The experimenter fills in the first columns **before** running; the evaluator fills in the result. Negative results stay. Scores are dev nDCG@10 unless stated.

## Protocol notes

- **Label rounds.** r0 = labels as handed over. r1 = +31 pooled judgments of the baseline's unjudged dev top 10 (11 graded 2, 9 graded 1, 11 graded 0), evaluator round 1. r2 = +127 pooled judgments of experiment 1's runs (3 graded 2, 32 graded 1, 92 graded 0), evaluator round 2. r3 = +110 pooled judgments of experiments 2–8 (6/21/83), evaluator round 3. r4 = +60 pooled judgments of experiment 9's runs (2/16/42), judged without the enrichment text, evaluator round 4. r5 = +56 pooled judgments of experiments 13–16 (7/13/36), evaluator round 5. r6 = +73 pooled judgments of experiments 10–12 (5/25/43), evaluator round 6. r7 = +10 pooled judgments of experiment 9b (1/1/8), evaluator round 7. r8 = +57 pooled judgments of experiments 17–20 (4/11/42), evaluator round 8. r9 = +17 pooled judgments of experiments 23–24 (2/4/11), evaluator round 9. r10 = +26 pooled judgments of experiments 21–22 (2/9/15), evaluator round 10 (r3 was pooled although no run exceeded 0.15, to keep new methods from being penalised by leftover unjudged items). Every result names its label round; comparisons use the same round.
- **MU presence leaks labels.** Muse described 5% of the corpus but 95% of the labelled answers. Moving MU items first, with no MU text, gives +0.156 (see `results/muse-ceiling.md`). Any method that happens to favour "the kind of items Muse described" looks better than it is; pooled judging of new top-10 items is the correction, so evaluators pool whenever `unjudged10` > 0.15.
- **Rule notes.** (1) Evaluator round 1 read MU text for the inventory before blind-judging the r1 pool: a small contamination risk; later judging rounds use evaluators that have not read MU. (2) For more-like-this lines the judging sheet has no query text, so the r1 judge judged against the example items listed in `queries.jsonl`. (3) One Gemini diagnostic call bypassed `lib/ai.py` (logged by hand; see STATE.md). (4) Evaluator rounds r3 and r4 wrote judging conventions that quoted five search texts next to grading rules into their results notes; they were committed and pushed, then redacted in the final commit. The earlier commits on this branch still contain them, so **squash-merge** this branch (or drop those lines from history) to keep them out of `main`.
- **Honest base from experiment 1 on:** the real gpt-5-nano rewrite (qr-v1). Its three repeats differ a lot per search (mean per-search range 0.13), so later experiments run on all three cached rewrite sets and report the mean paired Δ against the same-repeat base.
- **New base B2 from round r7:** experiment 9b (honest base + full-corpus tier-1 enrichment as separate BM25 + vector lists): dev nDCG@10 0.549 on r7 labels (+0.059 [+0.016, +0.108] vs the honest base). Later experiments are compared with `e9b_full_r{k}`.
- **Orchestrator decision on experiment 20 (HyDE on B2):** adopted under clause 2. Its hypothetical captions are already produced (and paid for) by the qr-v1 rewrite call that the base makes, so the dollar cost is unchanged; it adds ~0.1 s of local embedding. If a later prompt drops the captions, HyDE would cost ~$0.05 per 1k searches and would be a near miss (Δ < 0.03). **Base B3** = B2 + HyDE (`e20_hyde_b2_r{k}`), 0.570 on r8 labels.
- **Experiment 18 (MLT plan on B2)** is not adopted by the overall rule (3 of 16 searches change) but gains +0.049 on every more-like-this search in every repeat: recorded as a per-kind option for the founders.
- **Test-split scorings used: 2 of 3** (#1 the phase-1 "all 22" reproduction; #2 the finalist milestone T1, after blind judging of 88 pooled test items, round t1).
