## Verdicts (rule: Δ ≥ 0.03 with paired CI > 0, or Δ ≥ 0.02 with W > L and no extra cost)

- **13 · qr-v2:** **Not adopted.** Δ +0.025 is under 0.03 and the CI spans 0. Clause 2 (W/L 10/3) fails on the extra cost (+$0.018/1k, +0.3 s). Near miss: the gain is consistent on topic searches, but repeat 2 is negative, R@100 drops slightly (−0.009), and the single-answer group drops (0.315 vs 0.333). Cheap enough to be worth combining or re-testing.
- **14 · rerank top 30:** **Not adopted.** Δ +0.016 [−0.007, +0.046], 6/4/6, and unstable across repeats (+0.033 → −0.001). It costs +$0.26/1k and +3.7 s p50 (it more than doubles typed latency). It is the best run on the single-answer group (MRR 0.606 vs 0.470), but overall gain per dollar is poor.
- **15 · MLT same-collection:** **Rejected.** Δ −0.002 overall and −0.013 on MLT (2/1/0).
- **16 · MLT plan:** **Not adopted by the letter.** Δ +0.020 overall (unrounded +0.0205), CI lower bound 0.000, W/L/T 3/0/13, but extra cost (≈ $0.27 per 1k MLT searches, +1.1 s per MLT search), so clause 2 fails and clause 1 fails. On the MLT kind it is the largest single gain of the round (+0.109, 3/0/0, R@100 +0.045) at negligible cost. With only 3 MLT dev searches this is a strong near miss, worth confirming on more MLT searches.
- **9 · enrichment (sample), re-scored:** still **passes clause 1** on r5 labels: 0.550, Δ **+0.059 [+0.019, +0.103]**, 13/2/1, stable per repeat. It remains the provisional finalist pending the full-corpus run (9b).
