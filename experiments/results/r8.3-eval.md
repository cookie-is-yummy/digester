**Flags on FY** (for the orchestrator):
1. **Within noise and fragile.** |Δ| 0.032 is below the MDE (0.045), Holm fails (0.069), and CI > 0 is lost without the two most favourable searches. Per repeat the gain runs +0.020 to +0.047. It clears the 0.03 bar by 0.002.

3. **Grades are not an issue**: 0% of the Δ comes from this round's pool; without any AI-evaluator grade it is +0.035 [+0.005, +0.075].
4. **Cost and latency.** It is the first adopted arm that adds an LLM call to the hot path: +28% cost on all typed searches and +1.2 s p95. The cost-optimal goal may weigh this against a gain within noise.

**Flags on TI** (for the orchestrator):
1. **The verdict rests on the flagged set.** The bias check (section 2) suggests an unbiased `ig_known_dev` gain of about +0.03 to +0.08. The no-overlap subset alone is +0.031 within noise.
2. **The YouTube side pays.** Dev yt −0.024 [−0.052, −0.002] 2/8 (Instagram share of yt top 10s 0.17 → 0.23) and `known_dev` −0.004 [−0.009, −0.001] 1/10 (Instagram items in top 10s 0.052 → 0.064). These are below their MDEs, but both CIs exclude 0.

5. **Untested together.** FY and TI were each tested alone against B8.2. FY refines the fused top 20 that TI changes, so their effects need not add up. Since TI's losses are on YouTube reads and FY acts only on YouTube reads, a combined run (or TI gated to Instagram reads) needs a fresh test, not a reading of these.
