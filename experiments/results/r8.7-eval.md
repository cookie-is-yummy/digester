  - The exceptions in substance are CI's two known-item losses. Both sit just below their MDE (0.019 vs 0.020; 0.022 vs 0.025), and both have CI < 0 in all 3 repeats.

**Flags for the orchestrator:**
1. **CI as built is a displacement list, not a re-find aid.** It fires on about 61% of typed searches and adds about one new item to the top 10. Where the target's creator could be in the list, the effect is flat (+0.003 / +0.001). Where it cannot, it loses (−0.106 / −0.055). A retry would gate it: add the list only when a top-5 creator is a name match, or when the top creator's score is well clear of the next one. It would also give the list's items a tie-break role rather than a 0.5 RRF list. Even the name-matched strata show no gain here (−0.005 on 109 `known_dev` searches), so the expected value of a gated retry is low.
2. **R2 says where the rewrite path's value sits.**
   - Cheap moves are fine on `known_dev` (−0.004; 6 moved unrefined searches flat).
   - On dev, the moved searches lose mainly the refine (−0.28 on the 5 that B8.5 refined; +0.07 on the 5 it did not).
   - On `ig_known_dev`, the rewrite itself pays (s10_2 −0.09 on 65 moved unrefined searches), on a te-biased set.
   - A narrower variant could take most of the saving at lower risk. It would move only YouTube-read short searches (the `known_dev` profile) and keep half-remembered descriptive searches (description words / vague reference; most of `ig_known_dev`'s moves) on the rewrite path. That variant is untested. The dev-mix saving would then be well under 30%.
3. **Refine re-sampling** again dominates CI's refined subsets (+0.003 `known_dev`, −0.006 `ig_known_dev`, −0.003 dev). Those subsets are not read for the verdict.
