## Description pass (Y9.1-D, `md-v1`)

- **Code:** `experiments/methods/c91_describe.py`.
- **Model:** gemini-3.1-flash-lite, the cheapest model available that takes YouTube URLs. gemini-2.5-flash-lite still answers 404 "no longer available to new users" (rechecked 9 Nov). gemini-3.5-flash-lite costs $0.30 per M video tokens, against $0.25 here.
- **Prices:** checked on Google's pricing page on 9 Nov. `lib/ai_prices.json` gains a checked-on date for both flash-lite models and the missing `input_per_m` for 2.5-flash-lite. No price changed.
- **Rule 6 (the LLM fills a form; code acts):** the model fills a strict 3-field JSON form (shown, sound, said). Code then:
  - checks the keys and types;
  - drops URL, e-mail and instruction-like sentences;
  - removes quotes longer than 4 words and @handles;
  - caps the total at 40 words, cutting in the order shown → sound → said.
- **Pilot first:** 5 videos ($0.051 for 2,203 s of video) gave a projection of $1.28 for all 150. That is under the $1.80 clip trigger, so no video was clipped.

## Ranking arms (`runs/r9.1/`, all on the int8 store, 602 searches per file)

- **Code:**
  - `experiments/methods/c91m_search.py`: `index` / `refine` / `run` / `compare`;
  - `experiments/methods/c91m_diag.py`: label-free diagnostics.
- **Searches:** dev 44, yt_holdout 11 (not to be scored), known_dev 381, ig_known_dev 150 and facet_dev 16, with 8 more-like-this searches unchanged. One file per arm per repeat.
- **facet_dev inputs:** its qr-v1 rewrites, query vectors, refine responses and timing are read (only) from `runs/rewrites/facet_dev/`. No other `runs/rewrites/<split>/` folder was read.
- **Description index:** 150 description texts, embedded with text-embedding-3-large at 1,024 d in float ($0.0008). BM25 uses `yt_lib.BM25`.

| Files | Arm | One variable vs Q8 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `q8ref_r{1,2,3}` | Q8 reproduced | none: this file is runs/r8.5/q8 + runs/r9/q8_facet_dev merged into one, for `--cmp` |
| `m_r{1,2,3}` | **Y9.1-M** (`m-v1`) | `fair_fuse(own, [te, media])`: the two media lists (BM25, dense) form a coverage-fair group with described items as the eligible set |
| `m2_r{1,2,3}` | **Y9.1-M2** (`m2-v1`) | Q8's fused score, plus the RRF of the same two media lists for described items only. No imputation. |

**Gates.**
- **Reproduction:** the runner's `q8` equals runs/r8.5/q8_r{k} and runs/r9/q8_facet_dev_r{k} on **602 of 602 rankings, 1,000 deep, in every repeat**. This held before any M call and again in the final files.
- **Determinism:** under PYTHONHASHSEED 1 and 2, `m` gives **602 of 602 rankings identical in all 3 repeats**, and `m2` gives **602 of 602 identical in all 3 repeats** as well.
- **New refine calls:** 123 for M and 116 for M2, both gpt-5-nano with the unchanged rf-v1 prompt, cached in `cache/r9.1-media/` ($0.022 in total). The refine never sees media text.

**Why M2 exists.** The M2 note was added to LOG before any score, from label-free diagnostics only. Adding a coverage-fair group imputes RRF_own × 2/2 to every item that is not described, which is all but 150. That doubles the own lists' weight against the te group, so **M changes the te weight as well as adding media**: two variables.

Label-free result: the number of searches whose top 10 differs from Q8, and among them how many have any described item in the top 10.

| Repeat 1 | dev (38 typed) | known_dev (381) | ig_known_dev (150) | facet_dev (16) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M: top 10 differs | 30 | 67 | **147** | 12 |
| M2: top 10 differs | 14 | 46 | 28 | 12 |
| Searches with a described item in the top 10: Q8 / M / M2 | 4 / 5 / 11 | 10 / 6 / 33 | 3 / 3 / 25 | 5 / 5 / 11 |

- **On ig_known_dev, M differs from Q8 on almost every search** even though no described item is involved, which is the te re-weighting. Read M's Instagram result as that, not as media.
- **M2 isolates the media evidence, but shows the pilot's coverage artefact.** Only 150 items carry descriptions, so a query's nearest described items always get rank 1–2 in both media lists. M2 therefore lifts some described item into the top 10 on 33 known_dev and 25 ig_known_dev searches where Q8 had 10 and 3. In a full rollout every item would compete in the media lists and this boost would not exist. **M2's losses on searches whose targets are not described are an artefact of partial coverage, not a cost of media.** The fair test is on the facet-tagged searches the pilot was built for.

**Facet-tagged searches.** These use the Phase-A tagger plus Derrick's facet_dev ticks, and the counts are for repeat 1, label-free. The searches tagged looks, sound or said number 6 in dev, 7 in facet_dev and 8 in the known_dev tagger sample.
- In dev and facet_dev, every one of them has a changed top 10 under M.
- Under M, the mean number of described items in their top 10 goes from 2.3 to 2.7 (dev) and from 2.3 to 3.1 (facet_dev).
- Under M2 it goes to 4.5 (dev) and to 5.0 (facet_dev).
- A described item reaches rank 1 on 4 of 6 dev and 4 of 7 facet_dev media-tagged searches under M2, against 1 under Q8.
- Repeats 2 and 3 are similar.
- None of this says whether those items are the targets. Only the evaluator's key can tell.

**Cost and latency per search.**
- $ per 1,000 searches is unchanged from Q8, at $0.056–0.057 across all typed searches: no query-time call, and the new refines are priced like the old ones.
- The media lists scan 150 rows, which costs nothing measurable.
- The local p50 figures in the run meta (0.7–2.2 s) were taken on a 4-core box at load 17–21, shared with the Track-1 builder. **Don't read them as a latency change.** The arms differ only by two lists over 150 items.
