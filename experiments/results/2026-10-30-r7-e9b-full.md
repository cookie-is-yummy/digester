## Verdicts (rule: Δ ≥ 0.03 with paired CI > 0, or Δ ≥ 0.02 with W > L and no extra cost)

- **9b main arm: ADOPT.** It passes clause 1: Δ **+0.059** with paired 95% CI [+0.016, +0.108], W/L/T 13/3/0, and all three repeats are ≥ +0.052. Clause 2 does not apply because the method has a cost. Experiment 9's provisional adoption is now confirmed on the full corpus. **`e9b_full_r{1,2,3}` becomes the new base B2** for later experiments: pair repeat k with `e9b_full_r{k}`.
  - Caveats: R@100 gain is ≈ 0 (+0.001). The place search regresses. The single-answer group improves on nDCG/MRR (0.371/0.506 vs 0.333/0.470) but loses some R@100 (0.611 vs 0.667).
- **9b BM25-only: passes clause 1 as well** (+0.047 [+0.013, +0.088], 11/4/1), with the better R@100 (+0.013, CI > 0) and no query-latency cost. It is the fallback if the vector index's ≈ 0.77 KB/item or +0.05 s matters. The main arm beats it by +0.012 [−0.009, +0.034], which is not significant.
- **9 sample index:** superseded. The full index matches it (Δ −0.001 [−0.009, +0.009]).
