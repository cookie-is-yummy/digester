## 4. What drives each result

**R (route-r1).** The rewrite-routed searches are *identical* to H0r8 on every set (Δ exactly 0). Every difference comes from the instant-routed searches.

| Set | Routed instant | Δ on routed [CI], W/L/T | Δ on not routed | I's Δ on R's not-routed searches |

| dev nDCG@10 | 8 / 38 typed (21%) | −0.027 [−0.091, +0.038], 2/6/0 | 0 (30 typed + 6 more-like-this) | −0.104 (30 typed) |
| `known_dev` mrr2 | 329 / 381 (86%) | −0.000 [−0.018, +0.016], 84/74/171 | 0 (52) | −0.008 [−0.030, +0.020] |
| `ig_known_dev` mrr2 | 56 / 150 (37%) | +0.015 [−0.056, +0.082], 25/14/17 (s10_2 +0.048) | 0 (94) | −0.025 [−0.080, +0.031] |

- On short YouTube keyword searches the rewrite neither helps nor hurts: instant ≈ rewrite, 84/74.
- The router sends the less damaging searches to the instant path. I's Δ on R's routed searches is −0.027 on dev, −0.000 on `known_dev` and +0.015 on `ig_known_dev`. On the searches R keeps on the rewrite, it is −0.104, −0.008 and −0.025.
- The dev cost sits in the 8 routed searches, mostly name and topic kinds. It is within noise: the MDE on those 8 is 0.12.

**H5d.**
- dev: the gain is all Instagram (+0.026, the same as round 7's H5 − H0 +0.026); YouTube −0.010.
- Creator search fires on only 3 dev searches (Δ +0.035 there). The 41 searches where it does not fire give +0.006.
- **`known_dev`: creator search is not the cause.** The loss is the same where it fires (247 searches, −0.018 [−0.036, −0.002]) and where it does not (134, −0.019 [−0.034, −0.006]).
- **The cause tracks the router's platform read (H3 calibration).** Changes in mrr2 by the platform the router read:
  - `known_dev` (all YouTube):
    - searches read as **ig**: −0.033 [−0.064, −0.002] (92 searches, 14/25);
    - read as yt: −0.017 [−0.032, −0.002] (226);
    - read as both: −0.002 (63);
    - Instagram items in the top 10 rise 0.080 → 0.102.
  - `ig_known_dev`:
    - read as **ig**: **+0.045 [+0.013, +0.082]** (70, 25/8);
    - read as both: −0.027 (54);
    - **read as yt: −0.232 [−0.364, −0.109] (26, 2/21).**
  - By router type on `known_dev`: creator −0.021 (222), topic −0.020 (100), format −0.011 (34). Every type loses a little, so the boosts and filters also cost a little beyond calibration.
- **Summary:** calibration helps when the platform read is right and costs a lot when it is wrong. On the known sets the read is wrong often (24% of the YouTube searches are read as Instagram, 17% of the Instagram searches as YouTube). Round 7 saw the same pattern.

**Flags on R** (for the orchestrator):
1. **The dev point clause is fragile.** Δ is −0.0095 on seed labels and −0.0076 with this round's grade-1s set to 0. Both fail the −0.005 clause. The CI clause holds in every variant (lower bound −0.018 to −0.022). Half of R's seed loss was removed by 2.2% of its relevant top-10 items that rest on one noisy judge's grades. Fresh judges run stricter than Derrick, which leans against R, not for it.
2. **The ≥ 30% cut depends on the search mix.**
   - The blended number is dominated by `known_dev` (381 of 578 typed searches, 86% routed).
   - On a dev-like mix R saves only 21% of cost and 4% of p50. That fails the 30% clause.
   - On `ig_known_dev` it saves 37% of cost but only 5% of p50.
   - Real typed searches look like `known_dev` (median 3 words), so the blended figure is the realistic one. Still, the saving belongs to short keyword searches only.
3. p95 barely moves (2.18 s vs 2.25 s), because the long-tail searches still take the rewrite.
