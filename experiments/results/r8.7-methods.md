## Arm definitions (as preregistered)

### Y8.7-CI · `ci-v1`

**Index.**
- **Which creators get a document:** each `creator_key` (platform + handle, else display name) with ≥ 2 library items.
  - That gives **12,484 creators: 9,966 YouTube channels and 2,518 Instagram accounts**, covering 86,492 of the 136,908 library items.
  - **78 mutual-follow creators with ≥ 2 items are excluded.** They have no document and no vector, and nothing about them was sent to any API. Their items are never in the creator list, and their fused scores equal B8.5's.
- **Document:**
  - display name, handle, the handle split into words;
  - top 10 hashtags (non-generic, ≥ 3 characters);
  - top 20 title/caption words (no stop words).
  - Counts are member items. Ties go by term, and every set is iterated sorted.
  - Length: p50 99 and p95 249 characters; 293k tokens in total.
- **Vectors:** text-embedding-3-large at 1,024 d. They are stored float16 in `corpus/emb/oai3l_1024_creators_ci1` and held as per-dimension int8 with their own scales (12.8 MB as int8). A BM25 index over the documents uses the shared tokeniser.

**Per typed search, on both paths:**
1. Rank creators by RRF (k 60) over two lists:
   - the dense list: the max over the search's own dense query rows, which are the same rows as the item dense list (query + phrases + HyDE blend on the rewrite path, raw query on the instant path);
   - BM25 of the search's BM25 string.
   - Ties go by creator key.
2. Take the **top 5 creators**. Their gated, filter-kept member items, ordered by the item's own dense score (the exact array the item dense list ranks by), form the creator list.
3. Add **0.5 / (60 + r)** to B8.5's fused score, with no imputation.
4. Then apply P's multiplier, cut to 1,000 and run the refine gate.

No new query embedding is needed, because the creator side reuses the search's 3-large query vectors.

## Deviations and notes

1. **None from the preregistration in the arm definitions.** K = 5, weight 0.5, the document fields, the ranking, the item order, the fusion, the friend exclusion, the route-r2 rule, the refine model, prompt, gate and cache rule are all as written.
2. **Store path:** the creator vectors are in `corpus/emb/oai3l_1024_creators_ci1`, the shared embedding directory. The LOG block says `emb/…`. Only the parent folder differs.
3. **R2's routed shares came out above the preregistered cost expectation on the known mixes.** I expected −5% to −30%; the result was −69% on `known_dev` and −77% on `ig_known_dev`, because R2 moves 76 of 94 `ig_known_dev` rewrite searches and drops two thirds of `known_dev`'s refines. These were expectations, not part of any rule, and the rule was not changed.
4. **CI fires much more widely than its name-match motivation suggests** (about 61% of typed searches gain a new top-10 item). This follows from the ungated, every-search design the round asked for. It is not a deviation, but it means CI tests "creator-topic list on every search" more than "named-creator lookup".
5. **The CI diagnostic** (B8.5's pre-refine top 10 for the same search) is computed inside the search, timed separately and subtracted from local latency.
6. **A worker restart** happened during the refine pass. The pass had completed and cached all 203 responses before the restart, so nothing was re-paid. The WIP code and preregistration were committed by the orchestrator (16da485).
7. **Not seen:** no labels, no `eval_work/`, no per-search scores, no query, item or creator-document text printed or written in `experiments/`.
