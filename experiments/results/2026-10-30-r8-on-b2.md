## Verdicts (rule: Δ ≥ 0.03 with paired CI > 0, or Δ ≥ 0.02 with W > L and no extra cost)

- **17 · qr-v2 rewrite: NO.** Δ +0.007 [−0.025, +0.039], 7/4/5. One repeat is negative. R@100 drops by 0.019 (4/8/4), and P@10 and success@5 drop too. The place search falls further (0.208). On B2, the wider rewrite adds nothing measurable. Exp 13's +0.025 on the honest base does not carry over.
- **18 · MLT alike plan: NO by the letter.** Overall Δ +0.009 [0.000, +0.023], 3/0/13. It cannot reach the overall bar because it changes only 3 of 16 searches. It also has a cost, so clause 2 does not apply. On the **MLT kind** it is consistent: +0.049 (0.741 vs 0.692), 3/0/0, identical in all 3 repeats, R@100 +0.056. That is about half of exp 16's +0.109 on the honest base, because B2 already improved MLT. It is a real MLT-only signal with n = 3. Adopting it needs a per-kind decision or more MLT dev searches.
- **19 · gate-kind rejoin: NO.** Δ −0.008 [−0.035, +0.010], 4/4/8, and all three repeats are negative. R@100 +0.012 is not significant (W/L 2/4). The people search drops sharply (0.882 → 0.697). Said_shown R@100 rises (0.667 → 0.917), and so does the single-answer R@100 (0.611 → 0.694).
- **20 · HyDE in both dense lists: ADOPT under clause 2, on the condition that it adds no cost.** Δ **+0.022 [+0.008, +0.037]** (CI above 0), W/L/T **10/1/5**, and all three repeats are positive (+0.020 / +0.006 / +0.040). Most of the gain is on typed searches (+0.027, 10/1/2), led by topic, name, place and vibe. As run, the marginal cost over B2 is $0: the captions come from the same qr-v1 call B2 already makes, and the embedding is local. If the experimenter's production estimate (≈ +$0.05/1k searches for caption tokens) counts as extra cost, it is a near miss by the letter, since Δ is below 0.03. The orchestrator decides. Caveats: the audio search drops to 0.000 (B2 0.043), and the third repeat's query p50 was 0.53 s, which looks like an outlier.
- **B2 on r8:** 0.548 [0.428, 0.664], Δ vs honest base +0.059 [+0.016, +0.108], 13/3/0, R@100 0.518 (+0.010 [−0.015, +0.038]). B2 stays the base unless 20 is adopted on top of it.
