## Method

- **Scoring.**
  - All 12 round-4.3 files, plus `c0x`, `c1`, `d1` and `d4`, were scored on dev: 44 searches, 22 `ig` and 22 `yt`.
  - Each search's metrics are averaged over the 3 rewrite repeats.
  - CIs are 2,000-sample bootstraps over searches (seed 1). Comparisons pair repeat k with repeat k, average each search's Δ over the repeats, then bootstrap. W/L/T uses |Δ| > 0.005.
  - These are the same conventions as 4.1 and 4.2. Per-kind rows count a search under every kind it carries, so they overlap.
- **Pooling.**
  - Every unjudged dev top-10 item across the 4.3 runs and the references was pooled: 21 items over 12 searches.
  - 17 were Instagram items in YouTube-scope searches. 3 were Instagram items and 1 was a YouTube item in Instagram-scope searches.
  - Most came from E4, which pushes Instagram items into YouTube searches. E4's seed unjudged@10 was 0.02 (yt 0.04); every other run's was 0.00.
  - Backup before the merge: `evalset.pre_r4.3.jsonl`. Merged with `judge.py merge`, judge `claude-evaluator-r4.3`.
- **Blind judging, using 4.1's and 4.2's conventions.**
  - The sheet was built with the 4.2 builder (copied to `eval_work/r4.3/tools/`). It is grouped by search and shuffled within each search, with no run identity or rank shown.
  - Text shown: own text, interactions and duration. **te text was not shown.**
  - Items were graded by meaning, not by platform. Short-form items lost a grade where the intent asks for long videos or lessons.
  - Before grading, I checked the grades earlier rounds gave other-platform items on the same searches, for consistency. These were label reads only, with no run information.
- **Seed vs pooled.** On c4-r2 labels the Δs vs C0 were E1 +0.056, E2 +0.034, E3 +0.050 and E4 +0.047. The 21 judgments moved only E4 (+0.003). No verdict changed.

**E1: the widen-only guard fixes the narrowing bug and nothing else on dev.**
- On dev, its top 10 differs from D4's on exactly one search: the Instagram people/structure search where D4 had narrowed "saved or shared" to "saved only".
- Dropping the whole `require` returns that search to D1's ranking: +0.44 to +0.50 per repeat. Nothing else moves (1/0/43).
- So E1 is D1 on Instagram and D4 on YouTube. It keeps D1's Instagram te gain (own precision 0.83, ig-only projection +0.052) and D4's YouTube intent gains (behaviour +0.29, time +0.23, MRR +0.075).
- The unknown-watch widening never fired, as the experimenter reported.
- **Caveat:** this fix was designed from that one dev search, and the pass needs it: D4 without it does not pass. `yt_holdout` and `test` are the real check. E1 changes the top 10 of one `yt_holdout` search per repeat (from the experimenter's counts).

**E2: withholding te from descriptive titles works as designed, but it does not recover the YouTube loss.**
- The te share among descriptive YouTube items in the yt top 10 falls from 0.27 (D1) to 0.17, close to C0's 0.14. The descriptive share drops from 4.45 to 4.08 per top 10.
- **The freed slots go to thin YouTube items and Instagram items, at lower precision.** Thin items rise from 3.21 to 3.39 per top 10 (precision 0.46, their te share up to 0.26). Cross items rise from 2.39 to 2.59, and their precision falls from 0.42 to 0.39.
- The net on YouTube is +0.000 vs D1.
  - Topic, task and structure recover only +0.01 / +0.01 / +0.03 of D1's −0.05 / −0.06 / −0.09.
  - One people/shown/audio search loses −0.06.
- Instagram is unchanged by construction: no Instagram item is descriptive. The −0.007 there comes from YouTube items losing te rows and shifting the RRF ranks (4/5/13).
- In the projection, E2's yt-only score (0.501 vs `c0yt` +0.026, 13/3) is better than D1's (+0.015). The te reordering *within* YouTube helps slightly. What does not help is the extra Instagram items the change lets into yt searches.
- **The round-3 loss is not mainly te on descriptive titles in the top 10.** It comes from the te lists' influence on what fills the slots. 4.4 cannot fix it cheaply.

**E3 = E2 + guarded intent.**
- The intent adds the same three YouTube behaviour/time wins as in D4 (+0.032 on yt, 3/0/19). The widened guard removes the Instagram narrowing.
- It has the best yt-only projection of any arm that passes: +0.058 [+0.009, +0.119], 15/1.
- On the full ranking it loses some of that to the extra Instagram items (yt cross share 0.24 vs E1's 0.22).
- vs E1 it is −0.006, which cannot be separated on dev, at about 40% less te-pass cost (see below).

**E4: per-group BM25 changes the platform mix, not the ordering.**
- Fitting the own BM25 per group puts Instagram scores on a larger scale. Instagram items rise everywhere:
  - from 5.73 to 6.92 per top 10 in ig searches;
  - from 2.41 to 3.45 in yt searches, at 0.40 relevant vs 0.64 for YouTube's own items.
- The ig gain (+0.021) is fewer, weaker YouTube items in Instagram searches. The yt loss (−0.021) is more weak Instagram items in YouTube searches.
- Within each platform the ordering barely moves vs E3: the ig-only projection is 0.562 vs 0.565, and the yt-only projection is 0.541 vs 0.534.
- So the per-group statistics do **not** fix C0's −0.025 Instagram ordering loss. The ig-only projection stays at +0.049 vs `c0ig`, the same as E3.
- The experimenter's caution is confirmed. Any future per-group BM25 needs rank-based or calibrated merging, not merge-by-raw-score.

## Problems with the runs

1. **The finalist's margin rests on few searches.** Removing the two best searches (one YouTube intent win, one Instagram te win) drops E1 and E3 below the bar. E2 falls below it with only one removed.
2. **E1's decisive fix was designed from one dev search** (4.2's counterfactual). On dev it changes only that search. This is dev-informed tuning, honestly preregistered, but the held-out splits must confirm it.
3. **E4's merge-by-raw-score** mixes incomparable score scales. Its result is a platform-mix effect, as the experimenter flagged before scoring.
4. **No `test` runs exist.** Every 4.3 and reference run covers dev + `yt_holdout` (55 searches) only. The Finish step needs E1, E3 and C0 run on the 13 `test` searches first. That needs fresh qr-v1 rewrites and qi-v3 intents, at a few cents of OpenAI.
5. **te text is still not shown to the judge.** This slightly disfavours every te arm.
6. **The `own_text_kind` prototype branches on platform**, which the experimenter disclosed. It is acceptable for an experiment, but it is not product-ready under AGENTS rule 4.
7. Otherwise the runs behaved as documented. The 4.3 runs contain all 55 searches at depth 1,000, and E1's top-10 changes vs D4 match the experimenter's counts.
