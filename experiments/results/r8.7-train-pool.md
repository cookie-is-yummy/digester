# Round 8.7 setup: training pool for learned fusion (X4)

8 Nov 2026. Written by an evaluator in the setup role, who built no method. Counts only: no query texts, codes or per-search detail.

## Verdict: no disjoint pool exists in the data, so X4 is skipped

- No training file was written. `data/all/train/` was not created.
- X4 (architecture review option A5) waits on product click logs, or on a fresh My Activity export (see the last section).

## Where `known_dev` / `known_test` came from

- **Source:** 762 labelled searches, `source` = "known-item: My Activity search->watch pairs", ids `k0000`–`k0761` with none missing. They split 381 / 381.
- The build script is not in the repo. The sets arrived pre-built in the round-8 upload, and only the labels survive: query, `searched_at` and relevant codes.
- **Raw search history is not in the environment.**
  - The 2,737 "Searched" records of the My Activity export (2014–2021, `youtube-takeout-inventory.md`) are not in `data/all/` or `data/`.
  - They are also not in any of the 8 uploaded archives; I checked their listings.
  - The combined corpus has no search record kind. Its `interactions.jsonl` holds watches, likes, saves, playlists, collections, views and shares only.
- **Label rule, re-checked against the corpus watch history:**
  - For 760 of 762 searches, the grade-2 target is the first YouTube watch after the search, and it falls within 10 minutes.
  - The other 2 are second-level timestamp ties.

## Why the 762 are effectively every pair the rule can produce

| Check | Count |
| --- | --- |
| YouTube watches before 2020 | 18 (2015–2019); they hold the targets or relevant items of the 14 pre-2020 known searches |
| YouTube watches 2020 → last known search (Nov 2021) | 19,088 |
| Of those, within 30 min of a known search | 3,553 (18.6%) |
| Watch sessions in that window (30-min gap) | 3,932, of which 494 touch a known search |
| YouTube watches after the last known search | 97,332 (search history ends in 2021, so these can never pair) |
| Distinct query texts among the 762 | 762 (0 repeats) |
| Distinct codes excluded by the target / relevant rule | 1,362 (739 distinct targets) |

**Reading:**

- **Searches and watches overlap only from April 2020 to November 2021.**
  - Before 2020 there are almost no watches.
  - After 2021 there are no searches.
- **The ids are contiguous and the split is exactly even,** which points to the whole eligible set rather than a sample.
- **Repeated searches were collapsed to one text.**
  - A real history of this size always has repeats, so the 0 repeats among 762 texts show the collapsing happened.
  - Any dropped repeat has word Jaccard 1.0 with a known search, so the near-duplicate rule excludes it anyway.
- **Possible residue, which cannot be checked here:** 2020–21 searches that the builder dropped for some other reason. They would also have to:
  - fall outside the 30-minute zones around the 762 searches;
  - not reuse any of the 1,362 excluded codes;
  - not share wording with the 762 searches.
- **There is no evidence such a residue exists.** Even if it did, it is unlikely to reach about 300 pairs, because the source holds 2,737 searches in all across 2014–2021.

## Filters that would apply if a raw export is supplied (for consistency with `known_*`)

- **Auto-label:** the first video watched within 10 minutes is the target.
- **Disjointness from `known_dev` ∪ `known_test`:**
  - no search within 30 minutes of a known search;
  - no target that is a target or relevant item of a known search;
  - no word Jaccard ≥ 0.5 with any known search.
- **Autoplay (watch starting ≤ 15 s after the previous video ends):** cannot be applied.
  - The corpus has no video durations.
  - The audit found no autoplay targets in its hand check: median search → watch 12.5 s.
  - So `known_*` were evidently not filtered on this either.
- **Zero word overlap between search and target title:** **not** applied.
  - `known_*` keep the 33% of targets that share no word (validation audit §6).
  - The audit warns that this cleaning would favour lexical arms.
- **Output shape:** `{id, query, target_code}` with no timestamps.

## What would unblock X4

- Re-upload `My Activity/YouTube/MyActivity.html` (or the importer's `searches.jsonl` built from it) to an evaluator-only path.
- Then re-run this check.
- Expect a small pool at best. Product click logs remain the realistic source.
