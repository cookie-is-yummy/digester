# Round r11: round-2 Q2 (library-aware rewrite, exps 25–27) and Q3 (speed, exps 30–34), against B3

Evaluator: fresh agent, round r11. Split: **dev** (16 searches: 13 typed, 3 more-like-this). Base **B3** = `e20_hyde_b2_r{1,2,3}`. Repeat k of each arm is paired with B3 repeat k. The single deterministic run `e30_instant` is paired with each of the three B3 repeats. Scoring uses `experiments/eval/score_multi.py`: each query's delta is averaged over the repeats, the 95% CI is a 2000-sample bootstrap over queries, and W/L/T counts a win or loss at |Δ| > 0.005. Cost and latency are taken from the task and the runs' `meta`. B3's latency was measured fresh this session: total p50/p95 2.18 / 3.05 s, $0.118 per 1k searches.
