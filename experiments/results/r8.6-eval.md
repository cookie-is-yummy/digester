- **Power.** No |Δ| reaches its MDE on a primary metric. All four primary-set results of both arms are **within noise**. FL's `known_dev` loss (−0.011, MDE 0.021) and s10_2 loss (CI < 0) are the most consistent: they are negative in all 3 repeats, and −0.015 without the 2 most favourable searches.
- **Holm** changes nothing: no raw p is below 0.36.
- **Rounds without adoption: 1** (round 8.6 adopts nothing; round 8.5 adopted Q8).

**Flags for the orchestrator:**
1. **WP measures the label more than the user's habits.** As run, its `known_dev` gain sits on targets whose boost comes from the label watch. Searches whose target is not boosted lose −0.014. A WP-style prior needs interaction timestamps, so that only interactions before the search count, before it can be tested on `known_dev`. Even then, the displacement cost on unboosted targets is visible now. A smaller cap, or a boost only among near-tied scores, would be the variant to try. Dev gives no sign of the hypothesised re-find benefit on judged relevance.
2. **FL: the title-only list does not beat the shared index, and the channel list as built hurts.** The channel list fires on 88–100% of searches, and those searches carry the loss. A retry would need a channel match that requires the whole channel name (or a stop-list / minimum idf), and it would keep the playlist and "by channel" text in the YouTube keyword side. On present evidence, per-field BM25 is low-value for `known_dev`.
3. **Refine re-sampling** dominates the refined subsets again. WP's dev and `known_dev` refined-subset deltas (−0.013 / +0.010) are re-sampling-sized.
