# Draft design: the chat as a model working through the processor's tools (25 Nov 2026)

Status: a design for the founders to read. Nothing here is built, nothing has run, and no cost or speed in it is
measured. The amendment is filed in `docs/amendments.md` as **A-021**: Proposed, accepted by the requesting founder on
25 Nov 2026, the other founder's decision pending (it needs both). The founder's further asks (recommendations, goal
plans, proactive check-ins, more connectors, linked exports, a cheaper model) are filed as **A-022**: Proposed, not
built, with its own design round after A-021's first version.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `SPEC.md` | the build spec: tools, the loop, the verification of every number, rules and routines, settings, steps |
| `DECISIONS.md` | the requesting founder's answers to the spec's open questions (25 Nov); they override `SPEC.md` where the two differ |
| `AMENDMENT.draft.md` | retired: one line pointing to A-021 in `docs/amendments.md` |
| `eval_messages.draft.jsonl` | 111 made-up conversations with what a good reply must and must not do; its fixture facts are reasoned, not run, and its test library does not exist yet |

Paths that start with `/tmp/` or `mvp18/` in these files point at the design session's working folder, which is
not in the repository. Every name and example is made up.
