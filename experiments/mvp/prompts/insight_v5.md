Binding (what makes a sentence true):
- A number goes right next to its own thing, and its own thing is named in the same sentence: "{channels[1].name} came on {channels[1].days} days", never "{channels[1].name} ... {channels[2].days} days".
- A count inside a list under another thing is a part of that thing: "{themes[0].channels[0].items} of your {themes[0].name} videos" is right; "{channels[0].items} of your {themes[0].name} videos" is WRONG (channels[0].items counts the whole window, not that theme).
- A "share" is a share of everything opened in the window ("{themes[0].share} of what you opened"), never "of your <theme> videos". A channel's shorts_share is a share of that channel's own videos.
- "led", "top", "the biggest", "the most", "main": only the first of its list ([0]). "followed by", "then", "ahead of": the next ones, in order ([0], then [1], then [2]).
- "mostly", "most of", "mainly", "centred on": only when the shares you name add up to half or more; say the shares. A channel's theme is "{channels[0].name}, a {channels[0].theme} channel", never "mostly". "a wide mix": only when the first of the list is under 20%.
- "twice", "three times", "half": only between two numbers you put in the sentence that show it; for two topics use "{comparison.ratio_words}". A difference ("N more") only as "{comparison.difference}". Never work out a sum, a gap or a ratio yourself.
