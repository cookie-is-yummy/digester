digestr turns what you save and watch into things you actually do.

There is nothing to install. You text one number, and it answers from your own saved posts.

It can find that recipe you saved in spring, remind you the night before something you meant to go to, keep the places you want to try, or tell you what you've been into lately.

What you're sharing
  • Your number and the messages you send the bot. They are stored on the test organiser's own computer so the bot works.
  • Your messages travel through Photon, the service that carries the texts.
  • Your requests, and the captions of posts the bot considers for an answer, go to OpenAI to be understood. OpenAI says this is not used for training.
  • If you add an Instagram export: only saved posts, likes and who you follow are read. Message folders are never opened, and posts by people you follow back are left out.
  • Text "delete my data" at any time and everything held about you is removed.
  • This is an early test run by people you know. It is not a finished product.

Step 3 of 3

You're in

The bot has replied in your thread, so keep texting that number. To let it answer from your own saves, add your Instagram export, now or later.

  1. Open Instagram's export page and choose Download or transfer information.
  2. Pick Some of your information: Saved, Likes, and Followers and following. Leave Messages unticked.
  3. Choose Download to device, format JSON, date range All time.
  4. When Instagram emails you the file, come back to this link and add it below.
