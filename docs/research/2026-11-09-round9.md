# Round 9: is this processor good enough, and where not?

*9 Nov 2026 · one founder's library (21,293 Instagram items, 115,615 YouTube videos) · BRIEF-9: Phase 0, Phase A, Phase B, Track 1 rounds 9.1–9.3, the Track 2 media pilot, the Track 3 design, and the final held-out scoring. Aggregates only: no search texts, captions, titles, item codes or per-search scores. - **Spend:** about $2.52 of the $8 cap (ledger: OpenAI $4.692 → $5.685, Gemini $3.878 → $5.406). The media pilot was $1.28 of that.
- **Status:** nothing here is merged into `scrolldigest/`. A-005 was accepted for this round only. A-006 is still Proposed. A-007 (below) is new and Proposed.

## Bottom line

1. **Q8's Instagram gain does not clearly hold on real searches.** Every real-search reading is positive, but all are within noise. Derrick's 31 real Instagram searches: topic +0.039 nDCG@10 (MDE 0.080). His hidden sheet: +0.047 (MDE 0.091). Both together: +0.041 [+0.001, +0.078], Holm 0.047, so it fails α 0.025. The synthetic +0.110 is not what real searches show. **Direction confirmed, size not.**
2. **Every real-search gain rests on AI grades.** On Derrick's own targets Q8 is level (+0.010 target MRR on ig_real; −0.024 on facet_test's like searches).
3. **Known-item search is the clear gap.** Q8 puts the remembered item on the first screen for 30% of all 11 real exact searches [9%, 58%]. It manages 45% on real YouTube and 69% on synthetic Instagram. The proposed bar is 80%.
4. **The loop adopted nothing in three rounds (9 arms), so the stop rule fired.** Reading the search by intent (the qi-v1 frame) hurt when it replaced the typed words (−0.043 dev) and was neutral when added to them. The intent-aware refine alone (C2) came closest: +0.025 on facet_dev, 7/0, on AI grades only, at +36% cost.
5. **Much of the remaining loss is information we do not have.** On Derrick's Instagram sheet searches, 88% of the lost score is "information missing": how a post looks, what makes it good, or a caption too thin to name the subject. Query understanding cannot fix that.
6. **The YouTube media pilot was cheap and allowed, but did not show a fair gain.** It cost $8.51 per 1,000 videos ($382–1,460 for the whole library). Its looks gain was mostly a coverage artefact.
7. **Recommendation:** keep Q8. Do not make FM the default: on real searches it adds +0.000 to +0.010 at +90% cost. Spend the next round on measurement and data (Derrick's grades, real known-item searches, click logs, a coverage-fair media test), not on more query-side arms.

## 1. The question

BRIEF-9 changed the question from "which tweak wins on dev" to **"is this processor good enough, and where is it not"**. People search by creator, topic, type, looks, sound, what is said, tone, "examples of" a quality, and when they saw something. The round mapped which of these our data can answer, measured each, and tried to fix the largest gaps first.

**New this round:**
- **Real searches.**
  - `ig_real`: 31 real Instagram searches, each with Derrick's target.
  - `facet_dev` / `facet_test`: 16 + 15 searches Derrick wrote for sampled items, with his own facet ticks.
- **Two AI judges** from different model families (gpt-5.4-mini and gemini-3-flash-preview).
- **4-point grades:** wrong / related / good / exactly meant.
- **An "instance vs about" rubric line.**

**Judges vs Derrick (72 calibration items):**
- Relevant-vs-not κ is 0.60 / 0.45 per judge, and 0.55 combined. That equals round 8's single judge and does not beat it.
- Both judges are more lenient than Derrick.
- Judge B is below the 0.40 bar on Instagram (0.34).

*Metrics: dev and facet_dev are nDCG@10 (4-point gains); known_dev and ig_known_dev are mrr2. Every Δ is against Q8. Only C2 on facet_dev passes Holm (0.011), and it sits at its MDE (0.026).*

**What the rounds taught:**
- **Never drop the typed words.** A1 searched on the frame's paraphrase alone and lost most on wrapper-word searches (−0.080, CI < 0), the trap it was built for. Adding the frame (B3) removed the loss and the gain.
- **Creator lists added to the fusion displace targets.** Round 8's creator index lost this way, B1 nets −0.005 on its 99 known_dev firings, and C1's creator alias loses on the rewrite path. It is not a missing-data problem: handles and channels are 87–98% present.
- **Most "when" searches are about behaviour** (liked, saved, rewatched), not dates, so a time window misses them.
- **The personal vocabulary map is user data.** It belongs in the private vault, never in the shared store. It fired on 0 of 17 shorthand searches.
- **Not run before the stop rule:** search → look → refine (item 3), and type / tone / looks slot routing.

## 6. Track 2: media pilot

**Terms** (`experiments/research/r9-youtube-media-terms.md`):
- Sending public YouTube URLs to Gemini for a short description is **allowed for a research pilot** under the conditions recorded there (paid tier, public videos only, no download, no naming of people, no transcript).
- It is **unclear for the product**, for four reasons:
  - the feature is in Preview;
  - Google APIs Terms §5(b) on rights in submitted content;
  - YouTube's personal-use limit;
  - §5(e) on building databases.
- It needs a legal read.

**Run:**
- 150 videos (looks / sound / said targets plus matched distractors);
- gemini-3.1-flash-lite at low resolution;
- 0 rejected, $1.277;
- ≤ 40 words per video (shown, sound, "said" paraphrase, quotes ≤ 4 words).

**Cost:** $0.082 per hour of video; **$8.51 per 1,000 videos** at the pilot mix.

| Full YouTube library (105,090 videos) | List price | Batch API |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whole video, capped at 3 h | $1,460 | $730 |
| First 20 minutes | $929 | $465 |
| First 5 minutes | $382 | $191 |

Cost tracks video length. The 15% of videos over 20 minutes carry most of it.

**Why the gate was not met.** On 13 looks searches with described targets, M2 lifts the target's rank by +0.256 MRR (MDE 0.254). Formally that passes. But matched described distractors rose +0.176 on the same searches, because only 150 items had descriptions, so any described item jumped. The media-specific part (about +0.08) is below the MDE, and no graded cell reached its MDE. **No multimodal-embedding call was made.**

**A fair test** describes every candidate, say Q8's top 50 for about 20 real looks searches. That is about 1,000 videos: about $8.5, or $3–4 clipped to 5 minutes.

**Instagram (no media fetched; A-002 still Proposed), estimated from the audit:**
- For targets of Instagram looks searches, our text shows the look 29% of the time (36% counting hints). So for 64–71% of them the information is missing, and te `shown` cannot fix that because it restates the caption.
- About a third of real Instagram searches ask about looks. So roughly **one real Instagram search in five** needs visual information we do not have. This is a rough product of two small samples.
- **What an approved route would have to deliver to matter:** a short description of what is shown (subject, style, setting, on-screen text) and of the sound, indexed as one coverage-fair list. It must cover the thin-caption items (28% of Instagram), because that is where the missing information sits.

**On paper: visual or audio embeddings vs describe-then-embed.** Prices are from `experiments/research/mlt-multimodal-audio-media.md` (checked 30 Oct). The describe cost is measured here.

| | Describe-then-embed (Flash-Lite → our text index) | Cloud multimodal embedding (Gemini Embedding 2) | Local image embedding (SigLIP2 / CLIP) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost / 1k | YouTube $8.51 (measured); IG reel ≈ $0.65 (3 frames) to $1.34 (full) | 3 frames ≈ $0.36; 30 s video ≈ $23.70 | $0 |
| Facets | looks, sound, said, tone | looks only (audio inside a video is not embedded) | looks only |
| Storage | ~40 words + one 1 KB int8 vector | a second vector set | a second vector set |
| CPU-only / desktop | build in the cloud; search is CPU | cloud only, and every search needs a cloud query embedding | yes, 1.7–6.5 images/s on a contended CPU |
| One index? | **yes**: the same text space and lists | no: a separate space | no: a separate space |
| Privacy fit | readable, so it can be checked (no names, short quotes); "said" needs a rule-8 decision | opaque; can encode faces | opaque; on-device |
| Needs media we may hold | no for YouTube (URL route); yes for Instagram | yes | yes |

**Recommendation for a later pilot: describe-then-embed, coverage-fair.**
- It is the only option that fits the one-index design, can be read for privacy, and covers sound as well as looks.
- Run it on about 20 real looks searches from Derrick, describing all of Q8's top 50.
- Hold local SigLIP2 on keyframes as the desktop and Instagram option, once A-002 gives us media.
- Call a multimodal embedding API only if describe-then-embed shows a looks gain ≥ its MDE and still misses targets.

## 7. Final held-out result

Q8 stayed the finalist. H0r8, Q8 and FM were scored once on `facet_test` (scoring 1 of 3). The other held-out sets were re-read per facet and per trap, with no new scoring.

| Set | Q8 − H0r8 | FM − Q8 | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| facet_test like (12), nDCG@10 | +0.047 [−0.008, +0.102], MDE 0.091 | +0.000 | within noise; YouTube +0.118 (4), IG +0.012 (8) |
| facet_test exact (3) | target in top 10: 0 of 3 for every method | 0 | no method finds them |
| Real like, both sets (35, descriptive) | +0.041 [+0.001, +0.078], Holm 0.047 | +0.010 | fails α 0.025 |
| known_test_clean (216 YT), mrr2 | +0.003, MDE 0.041 | +0.008 | flat in every facet and trap cell |
| ig_known_test (150 synthetic IG) | **+0.110**, MDE 0.078 | +0.025 | only this set and its looks cell (+0.130) clear MDE |

- **The gain rests entirely on AI grades.** 99% of the relevant top-10 items on facet_test are this step's AI grades.
- **The judges cannot separate relevant from not on Instagram here.** Between the two judges, relevant-vs-not κ is −0.01 on Instagram items, because almost none gets a 0 from either (prevalence), and good-in-top-5 is 1.00 for both methods: a ceiling. This is judge against judge; against Derrick's calibration grades they score 0.60 and 0.34 on Instagram items. *(Clarified 10 Nov 2026.)*
- **The search quoted in BRIEF-9 changes nothing.** ig_real topic is +0.039 without it and +0.037 with it.
- **Scorings left:** 2 each on facet_test, ig_real, known_test_clean and ig_known_test.

## 8. Track 3: the user's direction as an optional input (design only)

**Shape.**
- One optional input: the user's **direction**, a few lines they write ("I'm building X", "this term I'm learning Y"). They can edit it, switch it off, or keep several and pick one.
- The planner passes it into the reserved frame field. The qi-v1 frame already has `direction`, always null in round 9.
- It may sharpen `subject` and `item_language` as **one extra, low-weight list** (weight ≈ 0.3, like round 9's extra-term lists). It may also give a small boost (≤ ×1.1) to items close to the direction.
- **It never filters, never removes or replaces typed words** (A1's lesson), and never touches the instant keyword path. Known-item searches cannot be told apart from the text (Phase A), so the no-harm check below is what protects them.
- Results it moved say so in `why_matched`.

**Softness.** The person's typed words always win. If the direction and the search disagree, the direction is ignored for that search. A check on searches unrelated to the direction must show no loss.

**Privacy.**
- It is user data, stored in the private vault.
- It is never sent to the shared content store, never used for another user, and never put in a cross-user cache. A rewrite cache keyed on the search text must also key on the direction, or skip caching.
- It goes to the model provider only inside the frame call, like the search itself, and is logged to `ai_runs` without its text.
- **It overlaps the existing `goals` table** (core, user-created). The founders should decide whether a direction *is* an active goal. One concept is better than two. Note that "Direction" already names goals-vs-attention in the glossary and the query API.

**How to evaluate it later.**
- **Searches:** written by the person whose direction it is. Each is scored with and without the direction, both top 10s are pooled, and the writer grades them. AI judges must be shown the direction, or they will grade the "with" arm as worse.
- **How many:** at the median per-search sd of 0.12 (round-8 audit), the MDE is about 0.116 at 10 searches, 0.075 at 21 and **0.050 at about 45**.
- **Plan:** about 45 on-direction searches (for example 3 directions × 15) to detect a +0.05 gain. Add about 20 off-direction searches for the no-harm check.
- **Grading load:** about 300–450 of the writer's grades if they grade the union of both top 5s.

**Reserved field.** `direction` is in the experiment frame only. Putting it in the query API is a contract change, so it is recorded as **A-007 (Proposed)** in `docs/amendments.md`.

## 10. What the founders must decide

1. **A-005: make it permanent?** It was accepted for round 9 only.
   - What earned its place: dev as a screen, held-out confirmation, and the human anchor.
   - The second judge did not raise agreement with Derrick (0.55, as in round 8). Its value is the disagreement flag, and it was blind on Instagram.
   - **Recommend:** keep A-005, with Derrick's grades as the binding part and a 20-search top-5 grading every round.
2. **A-006.**
   - **Items 1–2 (planner, Instagram te lists):** real searches now agree in sign (+0.041, not significant). Recommend accepting them in direction.
   - **Item 3 (creator index):** lost or was inert again. Recommend deferring it.
   - **Item 4 (refine):** keep nano optional. FM should not be the default.
3. **The media route.**
   - **YouTube via Gemini URL:** get a legal read, or a written answer from Google, before any product use.
   - **Instagram:** A-002 is still Proposed.
   - **Next media spend:** the coverage-fair pilot above (≈ $4–9), not the library ($382–1,460).
4. **A "what is said" summary under rule 8.**
   - The pilot kept a one-sentence paraphrase with quotes of 4 words or fewer, which is not a transcript. Real searches almost never ask for "said" (0–3%).
   - **Recommend:** don't keep it in the product for now. If you allow it, write the limit into `07-privacy-and-safety.md`.
5. **The research-only media descriptions** (150 videos, `data/all/media_pilot/`).
   - They must be deleted by **2026-12-09** unless approved. 22 contain possible names (unchecked).
   - **Recommend:** delete them. A fair test needs new, coverage-fair descriptions anyway.
6. **Also:**
   - accept, change or reject the scorecard targets;
